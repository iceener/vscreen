# Outcome

`vscreen shot` and `vscreen record` capture the virtual display (default) or one window through ScreenCaptureKit; any target outside the virtual display needs `--allow-main`, and a stopped display fails with `display_not_running` instead of falling back to Adam's screen. Landed on `vscreen-dev` (merge of 283a2ec, 9fddf72, 74bbc85); it also brought the wrapper's SIGINT/SIGTERM forwarding and the daemon `proc_name` fallback after a bundle reinstall. Known system behaviour: ScreenCaptureKit shows its "bypass the system private window picker" acknowledgement alert for vscreen (replayd `SCAlert`) on a capture until Adam clicks Allow; it was refused three times today (12:32, 12:57, 13:15), each time taking the front for 3–6 s. Signing with the hardened runtime (f74e68f) separately makes tccd deny the microphone queries ScreenCaptureKit issues. No independent review was bought (capture is read-only and the proof exercised it live).
